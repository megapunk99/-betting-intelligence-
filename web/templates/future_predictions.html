How Predictions Are Generated

Statistical Baseline

Every prediction starts with each team's average points scored. This anchors all projections in real historical performance.

Team Strength Adjustment

Home court advantage and team strength differentials are factored in to produce realistic edges against the market.

Quarter Splitting

Game totals are distributed across quarters using league-specific scoring patterns — NBA and WNBA have different ratios.

Best Bet Selection

Every quarter and half is analyzed for edge. The period with the strongest signal is highlighted as the recommended play.
